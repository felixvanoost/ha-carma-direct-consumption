# CARMA Direct Consumption

Import daily electricity and water usage from the CARMA Direct Consumption portal into Home Assistant. The integration creates two sensors per meter (`last_daily_usage` and `meter_reading`), imports historical usage as long-term statistics, and lets you assign a role to each meter.

See the the [README](https://github.com/felixvanoost/ha-carma-direct-consumption#readme) for complete instructions!
